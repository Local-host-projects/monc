Security positionDEMO BOUNDARIES ARE VISIBLE
Client-side card protection: the card package is encrypted in the browser and exported in a transferable QR token. MONC does not receive PAN or CVV. Production card charging still requires a PCI-compliant processor or bank tokenization partner.
OWNER HOLDS

The QR token, the 4×4 phone-style unlock pattern, and the ability to authorize. Transfer requires a verified recipient account.

SERVER HOLDS

An anonymous locator-indexed encrypted half, a public verification key, encrypted policy, and audit records. It cannot reconstruct the card alone.
