The one tenetMINIMIZE ENTRY · COLLECT FROM THE SOURCE
Design ruleMinimize data entry from both parties and collect directly from the sources in the most definitive / authoritative way. If a fact can't be confirmed at the source, MONC fails closed.
Security positionDEMO BOUNDARIES

Card details are encrypted in your browser; the key is derived from your pattern and never leaves the device. Authorization is separate from settlement, and the private key never leaves your browser. In the sandbox, Wema calls are deterministic — a real deployment swaps in the live gateway without changing the load-bearing parts.
